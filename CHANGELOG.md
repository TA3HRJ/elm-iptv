# Changelog

## 1.0 — first public release

ELM IPTV is an IPTV player for Fire TV and Android TV, built for the remote control, that also runs on
Android phones and tablets, Windows and Linux. It plays your own Xtream Codes or M3U playlists; it
provides no content.

## Before 1.0 (summary)

- **0.9.29 – 0.9.36:** phones and tablets in the same app; Windows and Linux versions; updates from the
  project's own server; the picture comes back on its own when it freezes while the sound plays; a hard
  limit on buffered data so long viewing cannot fill the memory; the error log says why a stream
  dropped; reconnecting stops, with an explanation, when the provider keeps closing the stream (another
  device on the same account); "Support ELM IPTV" in About with a QR code.
- **0.9.24 – 0.9.28:** live preview on the home screen with stream details; seamless switch between the
  preview and full screen; sound profile and decoder details; more film and series sorting; subtitle
  language labels; the channel list wraps from the last channel to the first; masked addresses and
  passwords in logs; hardened home-network page.
- **0.9.16 – 0.9.23:** headers and screens stay usable at every text size; text size changes must be
  confirmed; "Are you still there?" question before playback stops; TV guide edge keys; in-app updates;
  logos cached and quicker; memory and error logs for long-run diagnosis; finishing "Change order" no
  longer opens the channel.
- **0.9.10 – 0.9.15:** backup transfer from a phone on the home network (optional password); account
  details; settings in sections; film and series sorting; Turkish-aware search; daily background
  update; PIN attempt limit; automatic reconnect; audio delay; guide matching by hand; archive entry;
  clock in the header.
- **0.9.0 – 0.9.9:** profiles and kids profile; series recording; external player; multi-view; continue
  watching and watchlist; adjustable interface size; remote-friendly text fields; own channel order.
- **0.5 – 0.8:** parental lock and hidden channels; FFmpeg audio decoders (AC3, E-AC3, DTS); recordings;
  recently watched; adult category lock; programme search; frame-rate matching; start over.
- **0.1 – 0.4:** live TV with Xtream Codes and M3U; TV guide (XMLTV); reminders; catch-up; films and
  series; next episode.
